How to use the M32R
  1. Switch it on: POWER on the rear panel (top-left).
  2. Plug the sources into the rear panel (top strip): click an input socket IN 1-16. IN 1-5 already carry the presenter and guest lavaliers, music, a room mic and a 1 kHz tone. You can also plug your microphone / audio interface, an audio or video file or the audio of a browser tab (YouTube…). IN n feeds channel n.
  3. Pick the channel: press its SEL. The knobs at the top-left and the screen now edit that channel (the screen shows its name and its source).
  4. Condenser mics (CH 1-2) need 48 V (CONFIG/PREAMP).
  5. Raise GAIN (drag the knob up) until the meter next to it peaks around −12, never CLIP.
  6. Raise the channel fader to 0 and the MAIN fader (far right) to 0. MAIN STEREO must be lit and MUTE off.
  7. Listen with PHONES LEVEL / MONITOR LEVEL. SOLO lets you hear one channel alone; CLR SOLO clears it.
  8. Shape the sound: LOW CUT, GATE, COMP and the EQUALISER (pick a band, then FREQUENCY / GAIN / WIDTH). VIEW opens each section on the screen.
  9. Other channels: the FADER LAYER buttons change what the faders control (INPUTS 9-16…, buses, DCA).
  10. Monitor mix (bus): layer BUS 1-8 on the right, SEL a bus, press FADER FLIP (it blinks): the input faders are now the sends to that bus. SOLO the bus to hear it. FLIP again to exit.
  11. DCA: layer GROUP DCA 1-8, SEL a DCA, then Shift+click the SEL of the channels to add (on the real desk: hold the DCA SEL). The DCA fader moves them all.
  12. Mute groups: MUTE GRP (screen). Push an encoder = mute/unmute that group. Assign: Shift+click the encoder, then Shift+click channel SELs.
  13. Scenes: SCENES (screen). Encoder 1 picks the scene, push encoder 5 = Save, push 6 = Load (stored in this browser).

The row at the bottom tells you what is still missing for the selected channel to be heard. Hover any control to see what it does — and, on a channel, what is plugged into it.
